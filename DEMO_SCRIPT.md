# Lumen AI — Demo Video Script (60–90 Seconds)

**Target Duration**: 75 seconds  
**Tone**: Confident, fast-paced, Apple-keynote style product showcase  
**Visual Style**: Mobile screen recording inside clean device frame + desktop side-by-side  

---

### [00:00 – 00:12] Hook & Problem Statement
* **Visual**: Quick montage of app store download sizes (Photoshop Express 1.1GB, Canva 350MB), followed by a 375px mobile browser smoothly loading Lumen AI in under 0.5 seconds.
* **Audio / Narration**:
  > *"By 2030, generative AI will define creative editing. But today, billions of creators on budget mobile devices are locked out by bloated gigabyte apps and heavy cloud requirements. Introducing **Lumen AI** — professional-grade AI image editing engineered for the edge."*

---

### [00:12 – 00:32] Flow 1: 1-Tap Upload & Neural Object Removal
* **Visual**: Tap the "Alpine Vista" test preset. The mountain landscape loads instantly onto the high-DPI canvas. User slides the brush slider to 36px and brushes across the red surveillance drone in the sky with a rose mask overlay.
* **Audio / Narration**:
  > *"With zero installation and a client bundle under 76 kilobytes, Lumen gives creators full tactile control. Watch as we brush over this unwanted drone in the sky. Tapping 'Erase Selected Object' triggers our edge Poisson diffusion engine..."*
* **Visual**: Real-time progress bar sweeps from 0% to 100% in 42 milliseconds. The drone vanishes seamlessly, with natural sky and mountain textures synthesized in its place.
* **Audio / Narration**:
  > *"In just 42 milliseconds, the object is gone — boundary textures synthesized right on the phone's GPU, completely offline."*

---

### [00:32 – 00:54] Flow 2: Neural Style Transfer & Split Comparison
* **Visual**: Switch tab to "Style Transfer". Select the "Cyberpunk Neo-Tokyo" preset. Tap "Apply Neural Style". Progress bar maps color tensors and renders within 60ms. The interactive Split Comparison Slider emerges.
* **Audio / Narration**:
  > *"Next, creative transformation. Select from six curated neural style presets — from Japanese anime glow to Renaissance oil canvas. Applying Cyberpunk Neo-Tokyo maps multi-dimensional color tensors instantly. Creators can drag our interactive split comparison slider or hold down for an instant A/B check."*
* **Visual**: User smoothly drags the split slider back and forth, then taps and holds the "Hold for Original" button to reveal the original base image.

---

### [00:54 – 01:15] Flow 3: Revert, Export & Wrap-up
* **Visual**: Tap "Export HD". The toast notification confirms "HD Image exported to downloads". Tap the "Architecture" badge in the navbar to showcase the dual-tier edge-client + Hugging Face serverless pipeline modal.
* **Audio / Narration**:
  > *"Export crisp, high-resolution artwork with one tap. Lumen pairs client-side neural shaders with optional Hugging Face cloud models for zero-cost scalability. Democratizing generative creativity for the next 4 billion mobile screens. This is Lumen AI."*
* **Visual**: Final title card with Inter IIT Tech Meet 14.0 badge and GitHub repository link.
